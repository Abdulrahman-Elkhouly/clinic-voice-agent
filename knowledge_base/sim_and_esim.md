# Nova Mobile SIM and eSIM

## Getting, activating, or replacing a SIM

You can get a new Nova Mobile SIM at any Nova Mobile store or order one in the app for delivery in Qatar. A new physical SIM costs QAR 10. Bring your original Qatar ID if you are a Qatar resident, or your original passport and valid entry or residency documentation if you are visiting. The account holder must be at least 18 years old and present their own ID; a photo or photocopy is not accepted for in-store registration.

To activate a physical SIM, insert it into an unlocked compatible phone, turn the phone on, and follow the activation link sent by SMS. If the SMS does not arrive within 15 minutes, connect to Wi-Fi and finish identity verification in the Nova Mobile app, or visit a store with your ID. Keep the SIM packaging until activation is complete because it contains the SIM serial number.

For eSIM activation, first check that your phone supports eSIM and connect it to Wi-Fi. Open the Nova Mobile app and choose **Account > SIM management > Activate eSIM**. Verify your identity, select the number to activate, and follow the on-screen instructions to install the eSIM profile. If your phone asks for a QR code, scan the one displayed in the app using the phone's cellular settings. Do not share the QR code; it can be used to install your line. Once the eSIM connects, the previous SIM for that number is deactivated.

If your SIM is lost or stolen, call Nova Mobile on 111 immediately to suspend it, or visit a store with your original ID. A replacement physical SIM costs QAR 15 unless your postpaid plan includes one free replacement during its contract, as Connect does. The replacement keeps your existing number and deactivates the missing SIM. An eSIM replacement can be requested in the app after identity verification; the old eSIM profile stops working when the replacement is activated.

Only the registered account holder can request a SIM replacement. If you cannot access your account because the phone is lost, a store can verify your identity and help secure the line.

# Almahmoud Medical Center: Opening Hours

## Regular opening hours

Almahmoud Medical Center is open from Saturday to Thursday, 8:00 in the morning to 10:00 at night. On Fridays the clinic opens in the evening only, from 4:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night. Each doctor works specific days and hours within these times, so a particular doctor may not be available at every hour the clinic is open. The last appointment of the day starts 30 minutes before closing, at 9:30 at night.

## Friday hours

On Fridays, Almahmoud Medical Center is open from 4:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night. Friday mornings the clinic is closed. On Friday evenings, family medicine is available with Dr. Imran Qureshi, and the pharmacy and laboratory are open. Other specialties do not see patients on Fridays. Walk-in patients are welcome for family medicine on Friday evenings.

## Ramadan opening hours

During the holy month of Ramadan, Almahmoud Medical Center works shorter split hours. From Saturday to Thursday, the clinic is open from 9:00 in the morning to 2:00 in the afternoon, and again from 8:00 at night to midnight. On Fridays during Ramadan, the clinic is open from 8:00 at night to midnight only. Doctors' schedules change during Ramadan, so patients should check a doctor's availability when booking.

## Public holidays

Almahmoud Medical Center is closed on the first three days of Eid al-Fitr, the first three days of Eid al-Adha, and on Qatar National Day, 18 December. On the fourth day of each Eid, the clinic opens from 4:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night. On Qatar's National Sport Day, held on the second Tuesday of February, the clinic opens from 8:00 in the morning to 2:00 in the afternoon. The clinic's phone line and voice assistant keep working on public holidays, so appointments can still be booked for later dates.

## Laboratory hours

The laboratory at Almahmoud Medical Center takes blood and other samples from Saturday to Thursday, 7:00 in the morning to 9:00 at night, and on Fridays from 4:00 in the afternoon to 9:00 at night. The laboratory opens one hour before the clinic so that patients who need fasting blood tests can come early in the morning. During Ramadan, the laboratory is open from 9:00 in the morning to 2:00 in the afternoon, and from 8:00 at night to 11:00 at night.

## Pharmacy hours

The in-house pharmacy at Almahmoud Medical Center is open during all clinic opening hours: Saturday to Thursday from 8:00 in the morning to 10:00 at night, and Friday from 4:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night. The pharmacy fills prescriptions from Almahmoud Medical Center doctors and from other clinics and hospitals in Qatar, and also sells over-the-counter medicines.

## X-ray and ultrasound hours

X-ray and ultrasound services at Almahmoud Medical Center are available from Saturday to Thursday, 8:00 in the morning to 9:00 at night. X-ray and ultrasound are not available on Fridays. Ultrasound scans are done by appointment only, while X-rays requested by a doctor during a visit are usually done the same day without a separate appointment.

# Nova Mobile Billing and Payments

## Bills, payment methods, and charge disputes

Postpaid bills are issued monthly on the bill-cycle date shown in the Nova Mobile app. Payment is due 21 calendar days after the bill issue date. The app displays the due date and any outstanding balance. Prepaid customers pay when buying a bundle and do not receive a monthly service bill, though any separately purchased service may appear in their account history.

You can pay a postpaid bill in the Nova Mobile app, on the Nova Mobile website, by bank transfer using the account reference printed on the bill, or at a Nova Mobile store. Allow up to two business days for a bank transfer to appear. Keep the transfer receipt until the payment is shown as received. App and website card payments are normally reflected immediately.

If payment has not arrived within five calendar days after the due date, a QAR 10 late fee is added once to that bill. The fee is not charged if the full amount due is received during the five-day grace period. An overdue account may have outgoing service restricted until payment is received; paying through the app is the fastest way to restore service. A late fee applies per overdue bill, not per day.

If you think a charge is incorrect, open the bill in the app and select **Dispute a charge**, or call customer service on 111. Tell us the bill date, charge description, amount, and why you believe it is wrong. Please raise the dispute within 30 calendar days of the bill issue date so we can review the usage record. We will send an update in the app or by SMS when the review is complete. You must pay the undisputed part of the bill by its normal due date while the review is underway; disputing one item does not pause the rest of the bill.

For a bank-transfer payment that has not appeared after two business days, contact 111 with your account number and payment receipt. Do not send full card details in a message or support request.

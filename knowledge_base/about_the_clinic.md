# Almahmoud Medical Center: About the Clinic

## What Almahmoud Medical Center is

Almahmoud Medical Center is a private multi-specialty outpatient clinic in Izghawa, Doha, Qatar. The clinic offers seven specialties: family medicine, pediatrics, internal medicine, obstetrics and gynecology, orthopedics, dermatology, and ENT (ear, nose and throat). The center also has an in-house laboratory, X-ray and ultrasound services, and a pharmacy. Almahmoud Medical Center is an outpatient clinic only: it has no emergency department and no inpatient beds. Patients with a medical emergency should call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## Address and location

Almahmoud Medical Center is located at Building 27, Street 940, Zone 51, in Izghawa, Doha, Qatar. The building is on the service road of Al Shamal Road, on the right-hand side when driving north from Doha city. The clinic occupies the ground and first floors of a white three-storey building with a green sign reading "Almahmoud Medical Center". Reception, the pharmacy, and the laboratory are on the ground floor. The doctors' clinics, X-ray, and ultrasound rooms are on the first floor.

## Parking at Almahmoud Medical Center

Almahmoud Medical Center has free underground parking for patients and visitors, with 80 spaces. The parking entrance is on the side street to the left of the main building entrance. Six parking spaces next to the elevator are reserved for patients with disabilities, and four are reserved for pregnant women. Parking is free for the whole visit and no ticket is needed. When the underground parking is full, street parking is available on Street 940.

Calls to this number are answered 24 hours a day by the clinic's voice assistant, which can answer questions, register new patients, and book, change, or cancel appointments. The clinic's WhatsApp number is 5500 1234; WhatsApp messages are answered by reception staff during opening hours. The clinic's email address is info@almahmoudmedical.qa, and emails are answered within one working day.

## Languages spoken at the clinic

Staff and doctors at Almahmoud Medical Center speak Arabic and English. Some doctors and staff also speak Hindi, Urdu, Malayalam, Tagalog, and French. Patients who prefer a doctor who speaks a particular language can ask for one when booking; each doctor's languages are listed with the doctor's details. Reception staff can help patients who do not speak Arabic or English find a staff member who speaks their language.

## Accessibility

Almahmoud Medical Center is wheelchair accessible. The main entrance has a ramp and automatic doors, and an elevator connects the underground parking, the ground floor, and the first floor. Wheelchairs are available free of charge at the main entrance for patients who need one during their visit. Accessible toilets are located on both floors. Patients who need extra help moving around the clinic can ask reception staff to accompany them.

## Facilities for patients

Almahmoud Medical Center has separate waiting areas for women and families on the first floor, a children's play corner in the pediatrics waiting area, and a breastfeeding room next to the obstetrics and gynecology clinics. Prayer rooms for men and women are located on the ground floor. Free Wi-Fi is available throughout the clinic; the network name and password are shown at reception. Drinking water is available in all waiting areas.

# Almahmoud Medical Center: Doctors and Specialties

## Family medicine

Family medicine doctors at Almahmoud Medical Center treat patients of all ages for common illnesses and general health. Patients see a family medicine doctor for fever, cough, colds and flu, sore throat, stomach upsets, minor infections, high blood pressure and diabetes follow-up, general check-ups, and health certificates. Family medicine is a good first visit when a patient is not sure which specialist to see. Three family medicine doctors work at the clinic:
- Dr. Khalid Al-Mahmoud (male), speaks Arabic and English. Saturday to Wednesday, 8:00 in the morning to 2:00 in the afternoon.
- Dr. Maria Santos (female), speaks English and Tagalog. Sunday to Thursday, 3:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night.
- Dr. Imran Qureshi (male), speaks English, Urdu and Hindi. Friday 4:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night, and Saturday and Monday 3:00 in the afternoon to 10:00 at night.

## Pediatrics

Pediatricians at Almahmoud Medical Center care for babies, children, and teenagers from birth up to 14 years old. Parents bring children to a pediatrician for fever, coughs and breathing problems, ear pain, rashes, stomach problems, growth and development checks, newborn check-ups, and childhood vaccinations. Children aged 14 and over can see a family medicine doctor instead. Two pediatricians work at the clinic:
- Dr. Layla Haddad (female), speaks Arabic, English and French. Saturday to Wednesday, 8:00 in the morning to 2:00 in the afternoon.
- Dr. Ahmed Fathy (male), speaks Arabic and English. Sunday to Thursday, 3:00 in the afternoon to 9:00 at night.

## Internal medicine

Internal medicine doctors at Almahmoud Medical Center treat adults aged 16 and over with long-term or complex conditions. Patients see an internal medicine doctor for diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, thyroid problems, ongoing tiredness, digestive problems, and conditions that affect several parts of the body. Internal medicine is also suitable for detailed adult health check-ups. Two internal medicine doctors work at the clinic:
- Dr. Samir Nasser (male), speaks Arabic and English. Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday, 8:00 in the morning to 2:00 in the afternoon.
- Dr. Priya Menon (female), speaks English, Malayalam and Hindi. Saturday, Monday and Wednesday, 3:00 in the afternoon to 9:00 at night.

## Obstetrics and gynecology

The obstetrics and gynecology (OB/GYN) doctors at Almahmoud Medical Center care for women's health and pregnancy. Patients see an OB/GYN for pregnancy follow-up, pregnancy ultrasound, period problems, pelvic pain, family planning and contraception, menopause, and routine women's health check-ups. Both OB/GYN doctors at the clinic are women:
- Dr. Huda Al-Kuwari (female), speaks Arabic and English. Saturday to Wednesday, 9:00 in the morning to 3:00 in the afternoon.
- Dr. Rania Saleh (female), speaks Arabic and English. Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday, 4:00 in the afternoon to 9:00 at night.

## Orthopedics

The orthopedic doctor at Almahmoud Medical Center treats problems with bones, joints, muscles, and ligaments. Patients see the orthopedic doctor for knee, back, neck, shoulder, or hip pain, sports injuries, sprains, suspected fractures that are not emergencies, arthritis, and joint stiffness. The clinic's orthopedic doctor can also give joint injections. One orthopedic doctor works at the clinic:
- Dr. Omar Benali (male), speaks Arabic, English and French. Saturday, Monday and Wednesday, 9:00 in the morning to 3:00 in the afternoon.

## Dermatology

The dermatologist at Almahmoud Medical Center treats conditions of the skin, hair, and nails. Patients see the dermatologist for acne, eczema, psoriasis, rashes and allergies, fungal infections, warts, hair loss, nail problems, and moles that have changed. One dermatologist works at the clinic:
- Dr. Noura Al-Sulaiti (female), speaks Arabic and English. Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday, 9:00 in the morning to 3:00 in the afternoon.

## ENT (ear, nose and throat)

The ENT doctor at Almahmoud Medical Center treats problems of the ears, nose, sinuses, and throat for adults and children. Patients see the ENT doctor for ear pain and ear infections, blocked ears and ear wax, hearing problems, sinus problems, a blocked or runny nose that does not go away, nosebleeds, snoring, tonsil problems, and a hoarse voice. One ENT doctor works at the clinic:
- Dr. Daniel Okafor (male), speaks English. Saturday, Monday and Wednesday, 4:00 in the afternoon to 9:00 at night.

## Choosing a doctor by gender or language

Patients at Almahmoud Medical Center can ask for a doctor of a particular gender or one who speaks their language when booking. Female doctors at the clinic are Dr. Maria Santos (family medicine), Dr. Layla Haddad (pediatrics), Dr. Priya Menon (internal medicine), Dr. Huda Al-Kuwari and Dr. Rania Saleh (both obstetrics and gynecology), and Dr. Noura Al-Sulaiti (dermatology). Orthopedics and ENT each have one doctor, and both are male. Female patients can ask for a female nurse to be present during any examination.

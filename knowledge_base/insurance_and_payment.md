# Almahmoud Medical Center: Insurance and Payment

## Accepted insurance providers

Almahmoud Medical Center accepts health insurance from five providers:
- Gulf Shield Insurance
- Doha Care Health
- Pearl Medical Assurance
- Al Waha Insurance
- Nexa Health

Being an accepted provider means the clinic can bill the insurer directly. What a patient's insurance actually covers, and how much the patient pays, depends on the patient's own insurance policy and is decided by the insurance company, not by the clinic.

## What insured patients need to bring

Insured patients at Almahmoud Medical Center must bring their valid insurance card and their Qatar ID (or passport for visitors) to every visit. Without a valid insurance card, the patient pays the self-pay price and can ask their insurance company for a refund later. If the insurance card has expired or the policy has changed, the patient should tell reception before the consultation.

## Co-payments for insured patients

Most insurance policies require the patient to pay part of the bill, called a co-payment. The co-payment amount is set by the patient's insurance policy, for example a fixed amount per visit or a percentage of the bill. Insured patients at Almahmoud Medical Center pay their co-payment at reception after the visit. The clinic cannot tell a patient the exact co-payment before the insurer confirms it.

## Insurance pre-approval

Some services at Almahmoud Medical Center need approval from the insurance company before they are done. Pre-approval is needed for ultrasound scans, joint injections, and any procedure costing more than QAR 500. The clinic requests the approval from the insurer after the doctor orders the service. Approval usually takes one to two working days. If the insurer refuses, the patient can choose to pay the self-pay price or not have the service.

## Self-pay patients

Patients without insurance, or whose insurance is not accepted at Almahmoud Medical Center, can pay for their visit themselves. Self-pay patients pay the prices listed for each consultation, test, and procedure. Payment is made at reception at the end of the visit. Self-pay patients receive an itemized invoice that they can submit to their own insurance company for a refund.

## Payment methods

Almahmoud Medical Center accepts cash in Qatari riyals, debit cards, credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, and American Express), Apple Pay, and Google Pay. The clinic does not accept cheques. All payments are made at reception on the ground floor.

## Refunds

If a patient pays for a test or procedure at Almahmoud Medical Center and it is not performed, the full amount is refunded. Refunds are made to the original payment method within 14 days; cash payments are refunded in cash at reception. Consultation fees are not refundable once the patient has seen the doctor. Patients who believe they were charged incorrectly can raise it with reception or make a complaint through patient relations.

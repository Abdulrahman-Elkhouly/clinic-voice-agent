# Nova Mobile Postpaid Plans

## Postpaid plan options

Nova Mobile postpaid plans are billed monthly in QAR. The monthly allowance resets on your bill cycle date, and unused data or minutes do not roll over. Local minutes cover calls to Qatar mobile and landline numbers. International calls, roaming, premium numbers, and paid content are charged separately unless a plan detail below specifically includes them. All plans include voicemail and access to the Nova Mobile app.

- **Start** is QAR 80 per month with 20 GB of data and 300 local minutes. It has a 12-month minimum contract and includes caller ID at no extra charge.
- **Connect** is QAR 100 per month with 30 GB of data and 500 local minutes. It has a 12-month minimum contract and includes one free SIM replacement during the contract.
- **Flex** is QAR 120 per month with 30 GB of data and 700 local minutes. It has no minimum contract; service continues month to month until cancelled. Flex has the same 30 GB as Connect, with 200 more local minutes and a QAR 20 higher monthly price.
- **Unlimited** is QAR 180 per month with 100 GB of full-speed data, followed by unlimited data at reduced speed, and unlimited local minutes. It has a 12-month minimum contract and includes caller ID and voicemail.

Plan prices are recurring monthly charges and do not include optional add-ons or out-of-plan use. If you reach a data allowance on Start, Connect, or Flex, data access pauses until the next bill cycle unless you buy a data add-on in the app. On Unlimited, data continues at reduced speed after 100 GB; the reduced-speed service is intended for basic browsing and messaging. Calls beyond a plan's local minutes are charged at the current out-of-plan rate shown in the app before you place the call.

You can request a plan change in the app or by calling 111. A change takes effect on the next bill cycle, and any contract commitment already attached to your line continues to apply. The Nova Mobile app shows your bill-cycle date, usage, and available add-ons.

# Almahmoud Medical Center: Preparing for Your Visit

## What to bring to an appointment

Patients visiting Almahmoud Medical Center should bring their Qatar ID (or passport for visitors), their insurance card if they have insurance, and their file number. Patients should also bring any previous medical reports, test results, or X-ray and scan reports related to their problem, and a list of the medicines they currently take, including vitamins and supplements. Bringing the medicine boxes is also helpful.

## Fasting before blood tests

Some blood tests at Almahmoud Medical Center need fasting. For a fasting blood sugar test and a lipid profile (cholesterol), the patient must not eat for 8 to 12 hours before the test. Drinking plain water is allowed during fasting, but tea, coffee, juice, and chewing gum are not. Patients should take their usual medicines unless their doctor said otherwise. The HbA1c test, complete blood count, thyroid test, and vitamin D test do not need fasting. The laboratory opens at 7:00 in the morning so fasting patients can come early.

## Preparing for an abdominal ultrasound

For an abdominal ultrasound at Almahmoud Medical Center, the patient must not eat or drink anything except plain water for 6 hours before the scan. Fasting helps the doctor see the organs clearly. Patients with diabetes should tell the clinic when booking, because fasting can affect their blood sugar.

## Preparing for a pelvic ultrasound

For a pelvic ultrasound at Almahmoud Medical Center, the patient needs a full bladder. The patient should drink about one litre of water one hour before the appointment and should not go to the toilet until after the scan. Pregnancy ultrasounds in early pregnancy may also need a full bladder; the clinic gives instructions when the scan is booked.

## Preparing a child for a visit

For a child's visit to Almahmoud Medical Center, parents should bring the child's Qatar ID, the child's vaccination card, the insurance card if the child is insured, and any previous reports. A parent or legal guardian must come with any child under 18. For vaccination visits, parents can give the child a light meal beforehand. A favourite toy can help young children feel calm during the visit.

## Preparing for a dermatology visit

Before a dermatology appointment at Almahmoud Medical Center, patients should not apply makeup, creams, or nail polish to the areas that the dermatologist will examine. Patients with a rash that comes and goes can bring photos of the rash taken when it was at its worst.

## Preparing for an ECG

For an ECG at Almahmoud Medical Center, patients should not apply body lotion or oil to the chest on the day of the test, because it stops the sensors from sticking. Wearing a top that is easy to remove or open at the front makes the test quicker. The ECG takes about 10 minutes and is painless.

# Nova Mobile Prepaid Plans

## Prepaid plan options

Nova Mobile prepaid bundles are paid in advance and start when you activate them. Prices are in QAR. Local minutes cover calls to Qatar mobile and landline numbers; international calls and premium numbers are not included. Data and minutes expire at the end of the listed validity, and unused allowances do not roll over. You can check your remaining balance in the Nova Mobile app or by calling 111.

- **Mini** costs QAR 15 and includes 2 GB of data and 60 local minutes for 7 days. It suits occasional use or a short visit.
- **Smart** costs QAR 30 and includes 5 GB of data and 150 local minutes for 14 days. It has more than twice Mini's data and a longer validity, while keeping a modest top-up price.
- **Plus** costs QAR 50 and includes 10 GB of data and 300 local minutes for 28 days.
- **Max** costs QAR 55 and includes 10 GB of data and 450 local minutes for 28 days. Max has the same data and validity as Plus, but adds 150 local minutes for QAR 5 more.

The bundle price is charged when you activate it. A bundle renews only if you choose renewal in the app and your prepaid balance is sufficient; otherwise it ends when its allowance or validity runs out. You may buy another bundle before the current one expires, but the new bundle's validity begins at purchase and allowances do not combine into a rollover balance. If you use data after the bundle allowance is exhausted, standard pay-as-you-go rates may apply. To prevent that, turn off mobile data or purchase another data bundle before continuing.

To activate a bundle, open the Nova Mobile app, select **Prepaid**, choose a plan, and confirm the purchase. You can also buy a bundle at a Nova Mobile store. A new prepaid SIM costs QAR 10; required identity documents and SIM options are described in sim_and_esim.md.

# Almahmoud Medical Center: Appointment Policies

## How to book an appointment

Patients can book an appointment at Almahmoud Medical Center in three ways. The first is by calling the clinic's main phone line, where the clinic's voice assistant books appointments directly during the call, 24 hours a day. The second is by WhatsApp, where reception staff book appointments during opening hours. The third is in person at reception. When booking, the patient chooses the doctor or specialty, the day, and the time. Existing patients are asked for their file number; new patients are registered as part of booking.

## Registering as a new patient

Every patient at Almahmoud Medical Center has a patient file with its own file number. New patients can register by phone or at reception by giving their full name, date of birth, and mobile number. The patient receives a file number at registration and should keep it, because it is used for every future booking and request. At their first visit, new patients must show their original Qatar ID or passport so reception can complete the file.

## Walk-in visits

Almahmoud Medical Center accepts walk-in patients without an appointment for family medicine and pediatrics only. Walk-in patients are seen in order of arrival, between booked patients, so waiting times can be long at busy times. Specialist doctors in internal medicine, obstetrics and gynecology, orthopedics, dermatology, and ENT see patients by appointment only.

## Arriving for an appointment

Patients should arrive at Almahmoud Medical Center 15 minutes before their appointment time. New patients should arrive 20 minutes early to complete registration. Patients who arrive more than 15 minutes late may be asked to wait for the next free slot or to choose a new appointment, so that other patients are not delayed.

## Cancelling or changing an appointment

Patients can cancel or reschedule an appointment at Almahmoud Medical Center free of charge up to 4 hours before the appointment time. Cancelling or rescheduling can be done by phone with the voice assistant, by WhatsApp, or at reception. A patient who wants an earlier appointment than is available can ask to be added to the doctor's waiting list and is contacted if a slot becomes free.

## Missed appointments (no-show)

If a patient does not attend an appointment at Almahmoud Medical Center and does not cancel at least 4 hours before, a no-show fee of QAR 50 is added to the patient's next visit. Patients who miss three appointments within six months must pay the consultation fee in advance to book future appointments. The no-show fee does not apply if the patient was unable to attend because of a medical emergency.

## Booking for children and family members

A parent or family member can book an appointment at Almahmoud Medical Center on behalf of another patient. The appointment is made in the patient's own file, so the caller needs the patient's file number, or the patient's name and date of birth for a new registration. Children under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian at the visit.

## Choosing a doctor

When booking at Almahmoud Medical Center, patients can ask for a specific doctor, or for any available doctor in a specialty. Patients can also ask for a doctor of a particular gender or a doctor who speaks a particular language. A follow-up visit for the same problem is booked with the same doctor whenever possible.

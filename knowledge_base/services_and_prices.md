# Almahmoud Medical Center: Services and Prices

## Consultation fees

Consultation fees at Almahmoud Medical Center depend on the specialty. A new consultation costs QAR 200 for family medicine, QAR 250 for pediatrics, QAR 300 for internal medicine, QAR 350 for obstetrics and gynecology, QAR 350 for orthopedics, QAR 300 for dermatology, and QAR 300 for ENT. The consultation fee covers the doctor's examination only; laboratory tests, X-rays, ultrasounds, procedures, and medicines are charged separately. Prices are for self-paying patients; patients with insurance pay according to their insurance policy.

## Follow-up visit fees

A follow-up visit at Almahmoud Medical Center is a visit with the same doctor for the same medical problem. A follow-up visit within 7 days of the first consultation is free. A follow-up between 8 and 14 days after the first consultation costs half of the consultation fee. After 14 days, the visit is charged as a new consultation at the full fee. Follow-up visits still need an appointment, except for walk-in family medicine and pediatrics visits.

## Blood test prices

The laboratory at Almahmoud Medical Center offers common blood tests. Prices are: complete blood count (CBC) QAR 60, fasting blood sugar QAR 30, HbA1c (average blood sugar) QAR 90, lipid profile (cholesterol) QAR 120, thyroid test (TSH) QAR 100, vitamin D QAR 180, liver function test QAR 120, kidney function test QAR 100, and pregnancy blood test (beta hCG) QAR 90. Laboratory tests can be done with a doctor's request from Almahmoud Medical Center or from another clinic in Qatar.

## Urine and other laboratory test prices

Other laboratory tests at Almahmoud Medical Center include urine analysis for QAR 40, urine culture for QAR 120, stool analysis for QAR 50, and a throat swab culture for QAR 110. Laboratory test prices do not include the consultation fee. Patients with insurance should check whether their policy covers laboratory tests.

## X-ray prices

X-rays at Almahmoud Medical Center cost QAR 150 for each body area, for example a chest X-ray, a knee X-ray, or a hand X-ray. If two body areas are X-rayed in the same visit, each area is charged separately. X-rays are done on the first floor and need a request from a doctor. A radiologist report is usually ready within 24 hours.

## Ultrasound prices

Ultrasound scans at Almahmoud Medical Center cost QAR 400 for an abdominal ultrasound, QAR 400 for a pelvic ultrasound, QAR 450 for a pregnancy ultrasound, and QAR 350 for a thyroid ultrasound. Ultrasound scans are done by appointment and need a request from a doctor. Some ultrasound scans need preparation, such as fasting or a full bladder.

## ECG

An ECG (electrocardiogram), which records the electrical activity of the heart, costs QAR 100 at Almahmoud Medical Center. An ECG takes about 10 minutes and is done during a visit when the doctor requests it. The doctor reviews the ECG result during the same visit.

## Vaccinations

Almahmoud Medical Center gives vaccinations for children and adults. The seasonal flu vaccine costs QAR 120. Hepatitis B vaccine costs QAR 150 per dose, and typhoid vaccine costs QAR 180. Routine childhood vaccines follow the national vaccination schedule of Qatar and are given by the pediatricians; parents should bring the child's vaccination card. An administration fee of QAR 50 applies to vaccinations given without a consultation.

## Minor procedures

Almahmoud Medical Center performs minor procedures during clinic visits. Prices are: wound dressing QAR 80, stitches from QAR 250 depending on the size of the wound, stitch removal QAR 100, ear wax removal QAR 150, wart removal by freezing (cryotherapy) QAR 300 per session, and joint injection by the orthopedic doctor QAR 600. Procedures over QAR 500 need pre-approval for insured patients.

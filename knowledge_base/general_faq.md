# Nova Mobile General FAQ

## Common questions

**When are Nova Mobile stores open?** Most stores are open Saturday through Thursday from 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. On Friday, they are open from 4:00 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. Individual locations may vary; check the store listing in the Nova Mobile app before travelling.

**What is the customer service number?** Call 111 from a Nova Mobile line. From another phone, call +974 4400 0111. Support is available every day from 8:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. Qatar time.

**How can I check coverage at my home or workplace?** Open **Coverage** in the Nova Mobile app and search by area or map location. Indoor signal can differ from the map because of building materials, device capability, and local conditions.

**Can I keep my number when switching from another provider?** Yes. Request number transfer in a Nova Mobile store with your original Qatar ID and current number. Keep the old SIM active until the transfer completes, usually within one business day. Do not cancel your old service first.

**Can I use my phone as a hotspot?** Yes, tethering is available on Nova Mobile plans. Hotspot use draws from your plan's data allowance, including the same allowance limits and roaming rules.

**How do I check my data balance?** Open the Nova Mobile app and select your line. Prepaid customers can also call 111 to hear their remaining bundle allowance.

**Can I change my postpaid plan?** Request a change in the app or by calling 111. It takes effect on the next bill cycle; an existing contract commitment remains in place.

**Are international calls included in local minutes?** No. Plan minutes cover calls to Qatar mobile and landline numbers. International and premium-number calls are charged separately.

**What should I do if my phone has no signal?** Turn airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then turn it off and restart your phone. Check the coverage map and confirm that your line is active. If there is still no service, call 111 from another phone.

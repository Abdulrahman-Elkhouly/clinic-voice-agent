# Almahmoud Medical Center: After Your Visit

## When laboratory results are ready

Most blood and urine test results at Almahmoud Medical Center are ready the same day or within 24 hours. Vitamin D and thyroid test results take up to 48 hours, and urine, stool, and throat culture results take 3 to 5 days. Patients receive a text message when their results are ready. The clinic tells patients whether their results are ready, but results are explained by the doctor who ordered the tests, either at a follow-up visit or by the doctor calling the patient.

## Getting a copy of test results and reports

Patients can get a printed copy of their laboratory results, X-ray reports, or ultrasound reports from reception at Almahmoud Medical Center by showing their Qatar ID. A copy can also be sent by email to the email address in the patient's file, after the patient's identity is verified. Copies for another person, including adult family members, need the patient's written permission. Parents and legal guardians can collect results for their children under 18.

## Prescription refills

Patients at Almahmoud Medical Center who take regular medicines for a long-term condition, such as diabetes or high blood pressure, can request a prescription refill without a full visit. Refill requests can be made by phone with the voice assistant, by WhatsApp, or at reception. The patient's doctor reviews the request within one working day. If the patient has not seen the doctor in the last three months, the doctor will ask for a visit before renewing the prescription. Approved refills can be collected from the clinic's pharmacy.

## Sick leave certificates

Sick leave certificates at Almahmoud Medical Center are issued only by a doctor during a visit, on the day the patient is examined. Sick leave cannot be issued for days before the visit, and cannot be requested by phone without seeing a doctor. The certificate is printed at the end of the visit and can also be sent by email.

## Medical reports

Patients can request a medical report from Almahmoud Medical Center, for example for an employer, a school, an insurance company, or travel. Requests can be made by phone with the voice assistant, by WhatsApp, or at reception. A medical report costs QAR 100 and is ready within 3 working days. The patient is told when it is ready and can collect it from reception with their Qatar ID or receive it by email.

## Follow-up after a visit

If the doctor asks a patient to come back, the follow-up visit should be booked with the same doctor. A follow-up visit for the same problem within 7 days is free. Patients whose symptoms get worse after a visit can book an earlier appointment. Patients with emergency symptoms, such as chest pain, difficulty breathing, or severe bleeding, should call 999 or go to the nearest emergency department.

## Messages for a doctor and callback requests

Patients of Almahmoud Medical Center can leave a non-urgent message for their doctor, or ask the clinic to call them back, by phone with the voice assistant, by WhatsApp, or at reception. Messages for doctors are passed on the same working day, and the doctor or a nurse replies within two working days. Callback requests are returned by reception staff within the same working day during opening hours. Urgent medical problems should not be sent as messages.

## Complaints and feedback

Almahmoud Medical Center welcomes feedback and complaints. Patients can give feedback or make a complaint by phone with the voice assistant, by WhatsApp, by email, or in person at reception. Every complaint is passed to the patient relations team, which acknowledges it within one working day and gives a full response within 5 working days. Patients can ask to speak with the patient relations manager.

# Nova Mobile Roaming

## Roaming activation and travel packs

Before leaving Qatar, enable roaming for your line in the Nova Mobile app under **Services > Roaming**, or call 111. Roaming must be active before your phone can register on a partner network abroad. On arrival, restart your phone and select automatic network selection. Roaming charges are separate from your Qatar plan allowances.

Nova Mobile offers one-day travel packs in selected destinations. Each pack starts on the first eligible call or mobile-data session in that destination and lasts until midnight Qatar time. The included allowance is for use in the listed region only; it does not cover calls from Qatar or calls to premium numbers.

- **GCC pack:** QAR 25 per day in Bahrain, Kuwait, Oman, Saudi Arabia, and the United Arab Emirates. Includes 1 GB of data and 30 outgoing minutes.
- **Europe pack:** QAR 45 per day in France, Germany, Italy, Spain, and the United Kingdom. Includes 1 GB of data and 20 outgoing minutes.
- **Asia pack:** QAR 35 per day in Malaysia, Singapore, and Thailand. Includes 1 GB of data and 20 outgoing minutes.

The pack is charged on each day you use an included roaming service. It does not renew on a day when you make no outgoing call and use no mobile data. Allowances reset each day and unused data or minutes expire at midnight Qatar time. Incoming SMS messages are free. If a destination is not listed, a daily pack is not available there; standard roaming rates apply only if you enable pay-as-you-go roaming in the app. Otherwise, data and outgoing calls are blocked outside pack coverage.

To avoid unexpected charges, download maps and media on Wi-Fi, turn off data roaming when you do not need it, and use Wi-Fi calling only if your device and destination support it. Background app refresh can use data even when you are not actively browsing. Check your destination and remaining pack allowance in the app. You can disable roaming there at any time; disabling it prevents new roaming use but does not reverse charges already incurred.

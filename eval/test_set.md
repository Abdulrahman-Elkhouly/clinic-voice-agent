# Nova Mobile Voice Agent Test Set

## Answerable questions

1. **Question:** How much is the prepaid plan with 10 gigs and 450 local minutes?  
   **Correct answer:** Max costs QAR 55 and includes 10 GB, 450 local minutes, and 28 days of validity.  
   **Source:** `prepaid_plans.md`

2. **Question:** Is there a cheaper plan with the same 10 GB as Max?  
   **Correct answer:** Yes. Plus costs QAR 50 and includes 10 GB and 300 local minutes for 28 days, which is 150 fewer minutes than Max.  
   **Source:** `prepaid_plans.md`

3. **Question:** What does the QAR 100 postpaid plan include?  
   **Correct answer:** Connect costs QAR 100 per month and includes 30 GB, 500 local minutes, a 12-month minimum contract, and one free SIM replacement during the contract.  
   **Source:** `postpaid_plans.md`

4. **Question:** When does Unlimited slow down?  
   **Correct answer:** Unlimited provides 100 GB at full speed; after that, data continues at reduced speed.  
   **Source:** `postpaid_plans.md`

5. **Question:** What do I have to do before using my phone abroad?  
   **Correct answer:** Enable roaming in the Nova Mobile app under Services > Roaming, or call 111, before leaving Qatar.  
   **Source:** `roaming.md`

6. **Question:** I'm going to the UAE. What's the daily roaming pack?  
   **Correct answer:** The GCC pack costs QAR 25 per day and includes 1 GB and 30 outgoing minutes in the UAE and other listed GCC destinations.  
   **Source:** `roaming.md`

7. **Question:** When is my monthly bill due?  
   **Correct answer:** It is due 21 calendar days after the bill issue date shown in the Nova Mobile app.  
   **Source:** `billing_and_payments.md`

8. **Question:** How much is the late fee, and when does it apply?  
   **Correct answer:** A QAR 10 fee is added once per bill if payment has not arrived within five calendar days after the due date.  
   **Source:** `billing_and_payments.md`

9. **Question:** I don't recognize a charge. How do I dispute it?  
   **Correct answer:** Use Dispute a charge in the app or call 111 within 30 calendar days of the bill issue date. Pay the undisputed amount by the normal due date.  
   **Source:** `billing_and_payments.md`

10. **Question:** Can I activate an eSIM without Wi-Fi?  
    **Correct answer:** You need Wi-Fi. In the app, go to Account > SIM management > Activate eSIM, verify your identity, and follow the installation steps.  
    **Source:** `sim_and_esim.md`

11. **Question:** My SIM was stolen. What's the first thing to do?  
    **Correct answer:** Call 111 immediately to suspend it, or visit a Nova Mobile store with your original ID.  
    **Source:** `sim_and_esim.md`

12. **Question:** Can I bring my number over from another provider?  
    **Correct answer:** Yes. Request a transfer at a Nova Mobile store with your original Qatar ID and current number; keep the old SIM active until the transfer, usually within one business day.  
    **Source:** `general_faq.md`
